What Elbe Code stands for

Pragmatism over dogma: architecture must deliver business value. That is why Elbe Code combines strategic guardrails with actionable steps for product, platform, and engineering teams.

Transparent architecture decisions (ADRs) with clear trade-offs.
Scalable team structures and meaningful modularization.
Consider security, operations, and delivery from day one.
